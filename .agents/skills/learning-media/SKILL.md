# Learning media

Use the selected learner's checkout and read its [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) and [PROFILE.md](../../../PROFILE.md). Establish the requested lesson, audience, output, source revision and existing authorization. Follow [media workflows](../../../instructions/media-workflows.md) and only the relevant stages of [media prompts](../../../instructions/media-prompts.md).

Reuse the lesson's checked facts and recorded curriculum scope; fetch bounded requirements passages only when scope or depth needs resolving. Plan the learner's context and prerequisites before generating. A podcast needs an accessible conversational introduction; slides need a coherent sequence; an infographic needs a useful visual explanation. A printable study edition follows **Printable study notes** and the Markdown-based reviewable source, rather than being a second independently invented lesson.

For banners, infographics and precise diagrams use [learning-visuals](../learning-visuals/SKILL.md). Choose the representation by the learning task, preserving exact technical diagrams even when a supplementary generated illustration helps. Use only available, configured executors: the repository image and visual CLIs do not imply that speech synthesis, slide assembly or a print pipeline is installed.

Inspect the actual final image, pages or audio; process success and a transcript are not equivalent to perceptual review. Follow the workflow's acceptance, repair and stopping conditions, retaining the same request's cost and attempt history. If a required tool or artifact is unavailable, report what is complete and what still needs it.

Deliver through the configured destination and [handoff contract](../../../instructions/media-handoff.md). A media-only role may publish the authorized artifact links, descriptions and evidence; general lesson rewriting needs separate scope. Check the source revision again before insertion. [Drive operations](../../../instructions/install-drive.md) apply when that destination is configured. This skill installs nothing, creates no bot and grants no new publishing or spending authority.
